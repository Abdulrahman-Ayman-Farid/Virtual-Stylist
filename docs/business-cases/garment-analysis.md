# Garment Analysis: Understand Any Statement Piece

## Overview

The Virtual Stylist can look at a garment — a bold jacket, a striking dress, a statement top — and break it down for you like a professional stylist would. You give it a photo (or pick one of our sample pieces), and it tells you what the garment is, what colors it carries, how tricky it is to style, and how to make it shine.

## Problem

Statement pieces are exciting to buy and hard to wear. Most of us have at least one item hanging in the closet that we love but rarely reach for, because we're not sure:

- What actually goes with it.
- Which colors in it we should build an outfit around.
- Whether it's a "throw it on with jeans" piece or something that needs real styling effort.

Without a clear read on the garment, it stays unworn — and that's money and joy left on the table.

## Solution

The Garment Analysis feature gives you a stylist's-eye view of any piece in seconds. You can:

- **Pick a sample garment** from a curated sample wardrobe if you just want to explore.
- **Upload your own photo** using the file picker, your camera, or simple drag-and-drop (JPEG, PNG, and WebP images are supported).

Before the analysis runs, you can set your styling preferences so the advice fits *you*:

- **Fit**: relaxed, tailored, or oversized.
- **Style lens**: womenswear, menswear, or versatile.
- **Season**: spring/summer, fall/winter, or all-season.
- **Notes**: anything else the stylist should know (optional).

The AI then returns a full "Garment Decomposition" — a clear, structured breakdown of the piece.

## User Journey

1. Open the Virtual Stylist and find the garment upload area.
2. Either choose a sample garment from the curated sample wardrobe, or upload your own photo by clicking the file picker, using your camera, or dragging an image onto the drop zone.
3. Set your styling preferences: choose a fit, a style lens, a season, and add any notes you'd like the stylist to consider.
4. Start the analysis and wait a moment while the AI studies your garment.
5. Review your Garment Decomposition card, which shows:
   - The item name and category.
   - A styling difficulty badge: Easy, Moderate, Challenging, or Expert Statement.
   - A photo thumbnail of the garment.
   - An extracted color palette with swatches showing each color's name, hex code, and undertone (warm, cool, or neutral).
   - The silhouette, pattern type, and style tags.
   - The main styling challenge for this piece.
   - A stylist core tip you can act on right away.
6. Click any color swatch to copy its hex code to your clipboard — handy for shopping for matching items or sharing with a tailor or designer.

## Benefits

- Finally understand the statement pieces you already own.
- Get a professional-style breakdown in seconds, not a shopping trip.
- Know exactly how much effort a piece takes to style before you plan an outfit around it.
- Copy exact color codes so new purchases actually match.
- Learn your garment's undertones (warm, cool, or neutral) so your whole look harmonizes.
- Tailor every analysis to your fit, style, and season preferences.

## How to Get Started

1. Open the Virtual Stylist app.
2. Upload a photo of a garment you'd love to wear more (or pick a sample piece to try it risk-free).
3. Set your fit, style lens, and season preferences.
4. Run the analysis and read your Garment Decomposition.
5. Click a color swatch to copy its hex code, then head on to generate full outfits around the piece.
