# Stylist Chat: Talk It Through with Jean-Luc

## Overview

Sometimes you don't want a card or a checklist — you want a conversation. The Stylist Chat drawer connects you with **Jean-Luc, your editorial fashion director**: an AI persona with a candid, unfiltered point of view. Ask anything about your garment or your outfit, and get honest, opinionated answers in real time.

## Problem

Generic style advice tells everyone the same thing. What most people actually want is a trusted opinion: "Does this work?" "What am I getting wrong?" "How would *you* style this?" A chatbot that only flatters you isn't helpful — and one that doesn't know what you're wearing can't answer at all.

## Solution

The Stylist Chat drawer opens a conversation with Jean-Luc, an editorial fashion director who gives candid, unfiltered opinions rather than generic flattery. The chat is context-aware:

- It knows your **current garment analysis**, so it can speak specifically about your statement piece.
- It knows your **active outfit**, so answers relate to the look you're actually building.

Four starter prompts are offered as one-click chips to get the conversation going — for example, "Give me your completely honest, unfiltered opinion on this statement piece." and "What is the single biggest styling mistake people make with this silhouette?"

You can also choose from **three AI models** with a model preference selector, and use an optional **"force image design" toggle** when you want a visual. If you ask Jean-Luc to design or visualize a flat-lay — detected automatically, or triggered by the toggle — the reply includes a designed flat-lay right in the chat, complete with the image, the prompt behind it, a title, and the pieces. You can apply it as your outfit image with one click.

Messages show timestamps, the conversation auto-scrolls as you chat, and if the connection fails you'll see a friendly error message instead of a dead end.

## User Journey

1. Open the Stylist Chat drawer from your garment or outfit view.
2. Meet Jean-Luc, your editorial fashion director, and see the four starter prompt chips.
3. Click a starter chip — such as asking for a completely honest, unfiltered opinion on your statement piece — or type your own question.
4. Read Jean-Luc's candid, context-aware reply, grounded in your garment analysis and active outfit.
5. Optionally pick one of the three AI models from the model preference selector to shape the responses.
6. Ask Jean-Luc to design or visualize a flat-lay (or flip the "force image design" toggle) and see the designed flat-lay appear right in the chat.
7. Apply the designed flat-lay as your outfit image if you love it.
8. Keep the conversation going — messages are timestamped and the chat auto-scrolls as it grows.

## Benefits

- Honest, opinionated advice instead of generic flattery.
- Answers that know your exact garment and outfit — no re-explaining.
- One-click starter prompts when you don't know what to ask.
- A choice of three AI models to match your preference.
- Designed flat-lays appear right in the chat and can become your outfit image.
- A friendly, reliable experience with timestamps, auto-scroll, and clear error messages.

## How to Get Started

1. Complete a garment analysis and generate an outfit so the chat has context.
2. Open the Stylist Chat drawer and click one of the four starter prompt chips.
3. Ask Jean-Luc whatever you're wondering — honest opinions welcome.
4. Try asking for a designed flat-lay and apply it to your outfit if you like it.
5. Choose your preferred AI model from the selector to fine-tune the conversation.
