# Wardrobe Capsule: Keep Your Favorite Looks in One Place

## Overview

The Wardrobe Capsule is your personal reference collection. Whenever the stylist builds an outfit you love, save it to your capsule — then come back anytime to revisit it, load it back into the showcase, or download its flat-lay image.

## Problem

Great outfits are easy to lose. You generate three looks, love one, close the app — and next weekend it's gone. Without a place to keep your favorite looks, you end up re-generating, re-deciding, and re-doing work you've already done.

## Solution

The Wardrobe Capsule is a slide-in drawer that lists every outfit you've saved, with a count in the header so you know how many looks you've collected at a glance.

Each saved outfit card shows:

- The **occasion badge** (Casual, Business, or Night Out).
- A **flat-lay thumbnail** so you can recognize the look instantly.
- The outfit **title** and **mood**.
- The **number of coordinated pieces** in the look.

From any card you can:

- **View in Showcase** — loads the outfit back into the main showcase and closes the drawer, ready to explore, edit, or chat about.
- **Download** the flat-lay image as a PNG to keep on your phone or share.

A trash icon removes an outfit from the capsule when you no longer need it. And when your capsule is empty, a friendly message points the way: "Your capsule is empty — Save outfits from the stylist showcase to build your reference collection."

## User Journey

1. Generate outfits in the stylist showcase and find one you love.
2. Use the bookmark/save action in the showcase to add the outfit to your capsule.
3. Open the Wardrobe Capsule drawer and see your saved outfits, with a count in the header.
4. Browse your collection — each card shows the occasion badge, flat-lay thumbnail, title, mood, and number of coordinated pieces.
5. Click "View in Showcase" on a card to load that outfit back into the main showcase; the drawer closes automatically.
6. Click "Download" to save the flat-lay image as a PNG.
7. Use the trash icon to remove any outfit you no longer need.

## Benefits

- Your favorite looks are saved and always within reach.
- Recognize each outfit instantly with flat-lay thumbnails and occasion badges.
- Load any saved outfit straight back into the showcase with one click.
- Download flat-lay images to use while shopping or getting dressed.
- Keep your collection tidy with a simple remove option.
- A friendly empty state guides you to start saving from the showcase.

## How to Get Started

1. Generate an outfit in the stylist showcase.
2. Click the bookmark/save action to add it to your capsule.
3. Open the Wardrobe Capsule drawer to see your collection.
4. Use "View in Showcase" to revisit a look, or "Download" to keep its flat-lay image.
5. Keep saving the outfits you love to build your personal reference collection.
