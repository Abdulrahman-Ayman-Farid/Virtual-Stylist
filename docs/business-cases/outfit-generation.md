# Outfit Generation: Three Complete Looks, Ready to Wear

## Overview

Once your garment has been analyzed, the Virtual Stylist builds three complete outfits around it — one for Casual days, one for Business settings, and one for a Night Out. Each outfit is presented as a beautiful editorial flat-lay, laid out on textured linen fabric like a page from a fashion magazine.

## Problem

Knowing what a garment *is* doesn't tell you what to *wear with it*. Putting together a full look means juggling tops, bottoms, shoes, bags, and jewelry — and making sure the colors, materials, and proportions all work together. That's a lot of decisions for a Tuesday morning.

Most people end up defaulting to the same safe combination, or standing in front of the closet running late.

## Solution

The Outfit Showcase does the styling work for you. It presents **Three Complete Stylist Outfits**, each visualized as an editorial flat-lay on textured linen fabric. A simple occasion selector — Casual ☀, Business 💼, or Night Out 🌙 — lets you flip between the three looks.

Every outfit comes with:

- A **title** and a **mood**, so you know the vibe at a glance.
- A **numbered list of pieces**, each with its category (top, bottom, outerwear, shoes, bag, jewelry, accessory, or layer), name, color, material, and its own styling tip.
- A **color theory explanation** of why the palette works.
- A **balance rule** explaining how the proportions come together.
- A **stylist rationale** — the "why" behind the whole look.
- A **flat-lay image** so you can see the outfit, not just read about it.

And you can act on any outfit right away: copy the full checklist as formatted text, download the flat-lay image as a PNG, save the outfit to your capsule, open the edit studio to tweak the image, regenerate the image, or open the stylist chat to talk it through.

## User Journey

1. After your garment analysis is complete, scroll to the Outfit Showcase, titled "Three Complete Stylist Outfits."
2. Browse the first outfit, shown as an editorial flat-lay on textured linen fabric.
3. Use the occasion selector to switch between Casual ☀, Business 💼, and Night Out 🌙 looks.
4. For the outfit you like, read the numbered list of pieces — each one includes its category, name, color, material, and a per-piece styling tip.
5. Dig into the details: the color theory explanation, the balance rule, and the stylist rationale that explain why the look works.
6. Copy the full outfit checklist as formatted text to take shopping or get dressed from.
7. Download the flat-lay image as a PNG to keep on your phone.
8. Save the outfit to your capsule, open the edit studio to refine the image, regenerate it, or open the stylist chat with one click.
